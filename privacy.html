Privacy policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Armin Keihani is preparing a private SMS development test with his own mobile phone. This notice describes how information will be handled for that test.

Information and use

The test will handle the enrolled mobile number, consent and opt-out records, message contents, timestamps and delivery information. Armin will use this information to run, debug and secure the test and respect opt-out requests. Authentication information is used to verify access to the test. The test is limited to his own phone; it is not a customer messaging program.

Message frequency varies with testing. Message and data rates may apply.

Service providers

The planned service uses Twilio for SMS delivery, Cloudflare Workers and D1 to operate the service and store test data, and Firebase Authentication for owner sign-in. These providers process information to provide their services. Mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and requests

Test records are intended to be kept while the test is in progress and until the operator manually removes them during cleanup; there is no automatic deletion schedule. Cleanup requires separate steps for the test database and configuration. Twilio, Cloudflare and Firebase may retain records, logs or backups under their own retention practices, and cleanup of the test database does not by itself erase those copies.

For questions or a request concerning test data, contact team@museion.ai. Requests for deletion require manual review and handling of the separate service copies.
